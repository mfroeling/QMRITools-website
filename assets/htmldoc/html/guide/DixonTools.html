DixonTools

QMRITools package: Functions for Dixon data reconstruction and analysis.

DixonReconstruct — Performs Dixon reconstruction of real and imaginary data with B0 and T2* corrections (link and link).

DixonToPercent — Calculates the water and fat fractions from magnitude or complex water and fat images.

Unwrap — Fast 2D and 3D phase unwrapping (link and link).

OptimizeDixonEcho — Allow visualization of the optimal dixon echo spacing.

SimulateDixonSignal — Simulates signal of an Dixon gradient echo sequence.
