This toolbox adds the perceptually uniform [scientific colormaps](https://www.fabiocrameri.ch/colourmaps/){:target="_blank"} by Fabio Crameri to Mathematica (`AddScientificColors`) and can extract the color data of a colormap (`ExtractColorData`). The colormaps are used by the plotting functions of the [PlottingTools](/tool/plottingtools/). [Back››](/tool/)
