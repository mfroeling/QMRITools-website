This toolbox contains older functions that are kept for backward compatibility and are no longer developed. They are mostly import and export functions for older formats and data sources, such as DTItool and `.dat` files, Philips and Bruker diffusion data, physiology logs, and older fiber tract functions. For new projects use the NIfTI based functions of the [NiftiTools](/tool/niftitools/) and [ProcessingTools](/tool/processingtools/). [Back››](/tool/)
